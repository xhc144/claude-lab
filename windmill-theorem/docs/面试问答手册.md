# 模拟面试问答手册 · Fermat's Two-Square Theorem

面向清华新领军英语面试。视频第 10 章演示了其中 12 问；本手册给出完整英文参考回答、中文对照、考察点和可能的继续追问，另外补充 8 道视频之外更深的追问。

> **答题框架：结论 → 理由 → 钩子。**
> 先用一句话给结论，再给一个理由，最后留一个“钩子”（例如“the interesting part is the converse”），把考官引向你准备好的方向。
> 不要背诵。把每个回答练到能用自己的话、在白板上边画边讲。

## 0　一分钟版本（先背熟这一段，再展开）

> My favorite theorem is Fermat's two-square theorem: an odd prime is a sum of two squares exactly when it is one mod four. The easy direction is arithmetic mod four, because squares are zero or one mod four. The hard direction has a one-sentence proof by Don Zagier. You look at all triples with x squared plus four y z equal to p. Swapping y and z is an involution whose fixed points are exactly the representations we want. Zagier found a second involution with exactly one fixed point, so the set has odd size, and the swap must have a fixed point too. What I love is that the strange formula is really a picture: x squared plus four y z is the area of a windmill, and Zagier's map just cuts the same windmill in the other possible way.

我最喜欢的定理是费马平方和定理：奇素数是两个平方数之和，当且仅当它模四余一。容易的方向只需模四运算，因为平方数模四只能是零或一。困难的方向有扎吉尔的一句话证明：考虑所有满足 x² + 4yz = p 的三元组；交换 y、z 是一个对合，它的不动点恰好就是我们要的表示。扎吉尔找到了第二个对合，它恰好只有一个不动点，所以集合大小是奇数，交换也必然有不动点。我喜欢的是，那个奇怪的公式其实是一幅图：x² + 4yz 是一架风车的面积，扎吉尔的映射只是把同一架风车换一种方式切开。

## 第一层　陈述 Statement

### Q1. So, what's your favorite theorem?
**考察点**：开场表达、选题品味。
**参考回答**
> I'd say Fermat's two-square theorem. It says that an odd prime is a sum of two squares exactly when it's one mod four. What I love is the contrast: the statement is something you could explain to a middle-school student, but the proofs touch number theory, geometry, and even combinatorics.

我会说是费马平方和定理。它说一个奇素数是两个平方数之和，当且仅当它模四余一。我喜欢其中的反差：陈述连初中生都能听懂，证明却涉及数论、几何，甚至组合数学。

**要点**：一句话定理 + 一句话“为什么喜欢”。别一开口就讲证明。

### Q2. Can you state it precisely? What happens with two, or with composite numbers?
**考察点**：数学准确性、是否知道定理的边界。
> Sure. For an odd prime p, p equals a squared plus b squared for some integers a and b if and only if p is congruent to one mod four. Two is also fine, since it's one plus one. For composite numbers, the rule is: every prime factor that's three mod four has to appear to an even power. So forty-five works, but twenty-one doesn't.

当然。对奇素数 p，存在整数 a、b 使 p = a² + b²，当且仅当 p ≡ 1 (mod 4)。二也可以，因为二等于一加一。对合数，规则是：每个模四余三的素因子都必须出现偶数次。所以 45 可以（36 + 9），21 不行（3 × 7）。

## 第二层　证明 Proof

### Q3. Why is "one mod four" necessary?
**考察点**：基本功。
> Because squares are only zero or one mod four. So a sum of two squares is zero, one, or two mod four, never three. That direction takes one line. The interesting part is the converse.

因为平方数模四只能是零或一，所以两个平方数之和模四是零、一或二，绝不会是三。这个方向一行就够了，真正有意思的是逆命题。

### Q4. Can you sketch a proof of the converse, in about a minute?
**考察点**：证明结构是否清晰；术语 involution / fixed point / parity 是否用得自然。
> I'll use Zagier's proof. Consider the set S of positive integer triples with x squared plus four y z equal to p. It's finite. On S there's an obvious involution: swap y and z. Its fixed points give p equals x squared plus two-y squared. Zagier found a second involution with exactly one fixed point, namely one, one, and p minus one over four. Since an involution pairs up elements, the size of S has the same parity as its number of fixed points. So S has odd size, and the swap must have a fixed point too.

我用扎吉尔的证明。考虑满足 x² + 4yz = p 的正整数三元组构成的集合 S，它是有限的。S 上有一个显然的对合：交换 y 和 z，它的不动点给出 p = x² + (2y)²。扎吉尔找到了第二个对合，恰好有一个不动点 (1, 1, (p−1)/4)。对合把元素两两配对，所以 S 的大小与不动点个数奇偶相同。于是 S 的大小是奇数，交换也必有不动点。

**白板建议**：先写 S，再画“配对 + 落单”的点图，最后写 |S| ≡ #Fix (mod 2)。

## 第三层　条件 Conditions

### Q5. Where exactly did you use that p is prime?
**考察点**：最能拉开差距的一问——你是否真的读懂了证明。
> In two places. First, to make Zagier's map well defined: in the boundary cases, p would be a perfect square or a multiple of four. Second, to show the fixed point is unique: a fixed point means p equals x times x plus four z, and primality forces x to be one. For example, with twenty-one there are two fixed points, coming from twenty-one equals one times twenty-one and three times seven. So the parity argument breaks down, and indeed, twenty-one is not a sum of two squares.

两处。第一，保证映射有定义：边界情形 x = y − z 会给出 p = (y+z)²，x = 2y 会给出 p = 4y(y+z)，素数都不可能。第二，保证不动点唯一：不动点意味着 p = x(x + 4z)，素性迫使 x = 1。例如 21 有两个不动点 (1, 1, 5) 和 (3, 3, 1)，分别对应 21 = 1 × 21 与 3 × 7，奇偶论证失效——而 21 确实不是两个平方数之和。

**可以主动补充**：25 是另一个好例子：(1, 3, 2) 恰好落在边界 x = y − z 上，映射根本没有定义，因为 25 = (3 + 2)²。

### Q6. That map looks like a trick. How would anyone come up with it?
**考察点**：理解深度，“理解”与“背诵”的分水岭。
> That was my first reaction too. The answer is a picture. x squared plus four y z is the area of a windmill: a square with four rectangular blades. The outline of a windmill can be cut into a square and four blades in exactly two ways, and Zagier's map just switches between them. Once you see the picture, the formula feels inevitable.

我第一次看到时也这么觉得。答案是一幅图：x² + 4yz 是一架风车的面积——一个正方形加四片长方形风叶。风车的轮廓恰好有两种方式切成“正方形 + 四片风叶”，扎吉尔的映射就是在两者之间切换。看到这幅图后，公式就显得水到渠成。

**再深一步**：轮廓有四个凹角，每个凹角有两条边；沿其中一条边往里延长得到原来的切法，沿另一条得到新切法。只有十字形（x = y，即 (1, 1, (p−1)/4)）两种切法重合，这就是唯一的不动点。三种情形只是在记录新正方形变大、变小还是取 2y − x。

### Q7. Your proof shows existence. How would you actually find a and b for a large prime?
**考察点**：构造性意识、算法思维。
> There are two answers. The proof itself gives an algorithm: alternate the two involutions, starting from the cross-shaped windmill, and you'll walk to a solution. But that can take a long time. A much faster method is to find a square root of minus one mod p, and run the Euclidean algorithm on p and that number. The first remainder below the square root of p gives a, and then b follows. For thirteen: five squared is minus one mod thirteen, the first small remainder is three, and thirteen is nine plus four.

有两个答案。证明本身给出一个算法：从十字形风车出发交替使用两个对合，就会走到一个解，但可能很慢。快得多的方法是先求 −1 模 p 的平方根，再对 p 和它做辗转相除，第一个小于 √p 的余数就是 a，b 随之得出。以 13 为例：5² ≡ −1 (mod 13)，13 = 2 × 5 + 3，第一个小余数是 3，而 13 = 9 + 4。

**补充**：路径算法最多走 |S| 步，而 |S| 大约按 √p · log p 增长，对小素数没问题，对上百位的素数不可行；辗转相除只需要约 log p 步（Hermite–Serret / Cornacchia 算法）。求 −1 的平方根：任取模 p 的二次非剩余 c，c^((p−1)/4) 就是一个平方根。

### Q8. Is the representation unique?
**考察点**：知识面、能否从更高观点解释。
> Yes, up to order and signs. The cleanest reason is that the Gaussian integers have unique factorization. Writing a squared plus b squared as a plus b i, times a minus b i, a prime that's one mod four splits into exactly two conjugate Gaussian primes, so there's essentially only one way.

是的，在不计顺序和正负号的意义下唯一。最干净的理由是高斯整数有唯一分解：把 a² + b² 写成 (a + bi)(a − bi)，一个模四余一的素数恰好分裂成两个共轭的高斯素数，所以本质上只有一种写法。

**初等证法（备用）**：若 p = a² + b² = c² + d²，则 (ac + bd)(ad + bc) = p(ab + cd)，所以 p 整除其中一个因子；再用 p² = (ac + bd)² + (ad − bc)² 的大小限制推出两组相同。

## 第四层　延伸 Beyond

### Q9. How would you generalize the theorem?
**考察点**：数学视野，以及诚实。
> There are several directions. One is composite numbers, using the identity for products of sums of two squares, which is really multiplication of complex numbers. Another is other forms, like x squared plus two y squared, which works for primes that are one or three mod eight. And Lagrange proved that every positive integer is a sum of four squares. The deepest direction is x squared plus n y squared in general, which leads to class field theory. I haven't studied that yet, but it's something I'm really curious about.

有几个方向。一是合数：用“平方和之积还是平方和”的恒等式，它本质上就是复数乘法。二是其他二次型，比如 x² + 2y²，对应模八余一或余三的素数。拉格朗日还证明了每个正整数都是四个平方数之和。最深的方向是一般的 x² + ny²，它通向类域论。我还没学过，但非常好奇。

**注意**：没学过的就说没学过。“I haven't studied that yet, but…”比硬撑好得多。

### Q10. Is the theorem useful for anything beyond itself?
**考察点**：联系与应用。
> One example I like is Pythagorean triples: a prime is the hypotenuse of an integer right triangle exactly when it's one mod four. A more surprising one is pi. Counting lattice points in a circle with the two-square formula gives the Leibniz series: pi over four equals one minus a third plus a fifth, and so on.

我喜欢的一个例子是勾股数：一个素数是整数直角三角形的斜边，当且仅当它模四余一（13 = 3² + 2² 给出 5、12、13）。更令人惊讶的是圆周率：用两平方和的计数公式数圆内的格点，就得到莱布尼茨级数 π/4 = 1 − 1/3 + 1/5 − …

### Q11. If you had to keep only one idea from this proof, what would it be?
**考察点**：个人思考、知识之间的联系。
> Probably the parity trick: to prove that something exists, show that the number of such things is odd. It's surprisingly general. The same idea appears in the handshake lemma and in Sperner's lemma. And what I find beautiful is that a counting argument, which seems totally non-constructive, can be turned into an algorithm by following the pairs.

大概是奇偶技巧：要证明某个东西存在，就证明这类东西的个数是奇数。它出奇地通用，同样的想法出现在握手引理和斯潘纳引理里。我觉得美的是：一个看似完全非构造的计数论证，可以通过“沿着配对走下去”变成算法。

**联系你之前的作品**：斯潘纳引理里“从门进、从门出”的路径论证，和这里交替使用两个对合走路径，是同一个骨架；它们都通向布劳维不动点定理那一类“存在性来自奇偶性”的结果。

### Q12. Quick one: is 2027 a sum of two squares?
**考察点**：临场反应。
> No. Twenty twenty-seven is a prime, and it's three mod four, so it's impossible. But twenty twenty-six is: it's forty-five squared plus one squared.

不是。2027 是素数，而且模四余三，所以不可能。但 2026 是：45² + 1²。

**心算准备**：2025 = 45² = 27² + 36²；2026 = 2 × 1013 = 45² + 1²（1013 = 22² + 23²）；2027 是素数，2027 ≡ 3 (mod 4)。

## 附加追问（视频之外，难度更高）

**A1. Why is −1 a square mod p when p ≡ 1 (mod 4)?**
> By Wilson's theorem, (p − 1)! is −1 mod p. Pair k with p − k, which is −k: the product becomes (−1)^((p−1)/2) times ((p−1)/2)! squared. When p is one mod four, the sign is plus, so ((p − 1)/2)! squared is −1 mod p.

由威尔逊定理 (p−1)! ≡ −1，把 k 与 p − k ≡ −k 配对，得到 (−1)^((p−1)/2) · ((p−1)/2)!² ≡ −1；当 p ≡ 1 (mod 4) 时符号为正，于是 ((p−1)/2)! 是 −1 的平方根。（注意：这里又出现了“配对”。）

**A2. Do you know another proof?**
> Yes, a pigeonhole proof, often called Thue's lemma. Take r with r squared equal to −1 mod p, and look at the numbers x minus r y for x and y between 0 and the floor of the square root of p. There are more pairs than residues, so two of them agree mod p. Their difference gives small u and v, not both zero, with u congruent to r v. Then u squared plus v squared is a multiple of p, and it's strictly between 0 and 2p, so it equals p.

用抽屉原理（Thue 引理）：取 r² ≡ −1，考虑 0 ≤ x, y ≤ ⌊√p⌋ 时的 x − ry (mod p)，对数 (⌊√p⌋+1)² > p，必有两个同余，相减得到不全为零、绝对值小于 √p 的 u、v，满足 u ≡ rv，于是 p | u² + v² 且 0 < u² + v² < 2p，只能等于 p。

**A3. What happens to your set S when p ≡ 3 (mod 4)?**
> It's empty. x has to be odd, so x squared plus four y z is one mod four, never three. Zero is even, so there's no contradiction: the swap has no fixed points, consistent with the theorem.

S 是空集：x 必须是奇数，所以 x² + 4yz ≡ 1 (mod 4)。|S| = 0 是偶数，交换没有不动点，与定理一致。

**A4. Walk me through p = 5.**
> S has only one element, one, one, one. It's fixed by both involutions, and it says five equals one plus four.

S = {(1, 1, 1)}，它同时是两个对合的不动点，直接给出 5 = 1 + 4。

**A5. Why do you need S to be finite?**
> Parity only makes sense for finite sets. On an infinite set, an involution can have exactly one fixed point and still say nothing about anything else.

奇偶性只对有限集有意义。

**A6. Can you give other examples of involutions?**
> Complex conjugation, transposing a matrix, reflecting in a line, or taking the complement of a subset. In combinatorics, sign-reversing involutions are a standard way to prove identities by cancelling terms in pairs.

复共轭、矩阵转置、关于直线的反射、取补集。组合里“变号对合”常用来两两抵消、证明恒等式。

**A7. Is Zagier's involution unique, or could there be others?**
> On a given set there can be many involutions. What makes Zagier's special is that it has exactly one fixed point and it's explicit. The windmill picture explains why it is natural: every outline has exactly two dissections.

同一个集合上可以有很多对合；扎吉尔的特别之处在于它恰好一个不动点且完全显式，风车图解释了它为什么自然。

**A8. What would you like to learn next?**
> Quadratic reciprocity and the arithmetic of quadratic fields. The pattern "which primes split" in the Gaussian integers is the first example of a much bigger story, which ends in class field theory.

二次互反律和二次域的算术。“哪些素数在高斯整数里分裂”是一个大故事的第一个例子，这个故事最终通向类域论。
