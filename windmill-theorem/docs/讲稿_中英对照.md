## 04　核心思想 · The Key Idea

| 中文版旁白 | English narration |
|---|---|
| 直接构造 a 和 b 很难，因为我们根本不知道它们长什么样。扎吉尔换了一个思路：不去找，而是去数。准确地说，只数奇偶。 | Constructing a and b directly is hard, because we have no idea what they look like. Zagier's idea is: don't search, count. And not even count exactly, just count mod two. |
| 想象一场舞会。每个人要么和一位舞伴配成一对，要么独自站着。如果总人数是奇数，那就一定有人落单。 | Imagine a dance. Everyone either pairs up with a partner, or stands alone. If the total number of people is odd, someone must be standing alone. |

<details><summary>英文逐句释义（跟读用）</summary>

- Constructing a and b directly is hard, because we have no idea what they look like.  
  直接构造 a 和 b 很难，因为我们完全不知道它们长什么样。
- Zagier's idea is: don't search, count. And not even count exactly, just count mod two.  
  扎吉尔的想法是：不去找，而去数。甚至不用精确地数，只看奇偶。
- Imagine a dance. Everyone either pairs up with a partner, or stands alone.  
  想象一场舞会：每个人要么和舞伴配成一对，要么独自站着。
- If the total number of people is odd, someone must be standing alone.  
  如果总人数是奇数，就一定有人落单。
- In mathematics, this pairing is called an involution: a map f which, applied twice, brings you back to where you started.  
  在数学里，这种配对叫作对合：一个映射 f，作用两次就回到出发点。
- It splits a set into pairs, and the elements left alone are the fixed points, where f of s equals s.  
  它把集合分成一对一对，落单的元素就是不动点，即 f(s) = s。
- This gives a simple but powerful fact: the size of the set and the number of fixed points always have the same parity.  
  由此得到一个简单而强大的事实：集合的大小与不动点的个数奇偶性相同。
- Now the whole strategy is clear. Find a finite set S with two involutions on it.  
  整体策略就清楚了：找一个有限集合 S，在上面放两个对合。
- If the first one has exactly one fixed point, then S has an odd number of elements.  
  如果第一个恰好有一个不动点，那么 S 的元素个数就是奇数。
- So the second involution must also have an odd number of fixed points. In particular, at least one.  
  于是第二个对合的不动点个数也是奇数，特别地，至少有一个。
- If the fixed points of the second involution are exactly the sums of two squares we want, we're done.  
  如果第二个对合的不动点恰好就是我们想要的平方和表示，证明就完成了。
- So the questions are: what is S, and what are the two involutions?  
  所以问题是：S 是什么？两个对合又是什么？

## 05　集合与交换 · The Set and the Swap

| 中文版旁白 | English narration |

| 我们想要 p = x² + (2y)²，也就是 x² + 4·y·y。 | We want p = x² + (2y)². That is, x² + 4·y·y. |

<details><summary>英文逐句释义（跟读用）</summary>

- We want p = x² + (2y)². That is, x² + 4·y·y.  
  我们想要 p = x² + (2y)²，也就是 x² + 4·y·y。
- Here's the key move: relax the condition.  
  关键的一步：放宽条件。
- Replace y times y by y times z, and consider all triples of positive integers (x, y, z) with x² + 4yz = p. Call this set S.  
  把 y·y 换成 y·z，考虑所有满足 x² + 4yz = p 的正整数三元组 (x, y, z)，记这个集合为 S。
- Since x, y and z are positive and bounded by p, S is finite.  
  因为 x、y、z 都是正数且被 p 限制，所以 S 是有限的。
- For p equal to thirteen, S has just three elements: (1, 1, 3), (1, 3, 1), and (3, 1, 1).  
  当 p = 13 时，S 只有三个元素：(1, 1, 3)、(1, 3, 1) 和 (3, 1, 1)。
- The first involution is almost a gift: just swap y and z.  
  第一个对合几乎是现成的：交换 y 和 z。
- Its fixed points are the triples with y equal to z, and then p = x² + (2y)².  
  它的不动点是 y = z 的三元组，此时 p 恰好等于 x² + (2y)²。
- That's the beauty of relaxing: the thing we want has become the fixed point of a symmetry.  
  这就是放宽条件的妙处：我们想要的东西变成了一个对称性的不动点。
- For thirteen, the fixed point is (3, 1, 1), which gives thirteen equals three squared plus two squared.  
  对十三来说，不动点是 (3, 1, 1)，给出 13 = 3² + 2²。
- Now we need a second involution with exactly one fixed point.  
  现在我们需要第二个对合，它恰好只有一个不动点。
- Here is Zagier's formula, in three cases.  
  这是扎吉尔给出的公式，分三种情形。
- At first glance, it looks like it was pulled out of thin air.  
  乍一看，它像是凭空变出来的。
- But actually, it's a picture.  
  但其实，它是一幅图。

- For example, 2026 is two times 1013, a prime that's one mod four. So 2026 = 45² + 1².  
  例如，2026 = 2 × 1013，而 1013 是模四余一的素数，所以 2026 = 45² + 1²。
- But 2027 is itself a prime that is three mod four, so it can't be done.  
  但 2027 本身是模四余三的素数，所以做不到。
- The second direction is the Gaussian integers: complex numbers a + bi, with a and b integers.  
  第二个方向是高斯整数：形如 a + bi 的复数，其中 a、b 是整数。
- In this world, p = a² + b² means exactly that p factors as (a + bi)(a − bi).  
  在这个世界里，p = a² + b² 恰好意味着 p 能分解为 (a + bi)(a − bi)。
- Primes that are one mod four split, like 13 = (3 + 2i)(3 − 2i). Primes that are three mod four stay prime. And two is a special case.  
  模四余一的素数会分裂，例如 13 = (3 + 2i)(3 − 2i)；模四余三的素数仍是素数；而 2 是特例。
- The Gaussian integers also have unique factorization, and that's exactly why the representation as a sum of two squares is unique.  
  高斯整数也有唯一分解，这正是平方和表示唯一的原因。
- The third direction is other quadratic forms.  
  第三个方向是其他二次型。
- Apart from two, the primes of the form x² + 2y² are exactly those that are one or three mod eight. Apart from three itself, x² + 3y² gives the primes that are one mod three.  
  除 2 以外，形如 x² + 2y² 的素数恰好是模八余一或余三的素数；除 3 本身以外，x² + 3y² 对应模三余一的素数。
- Going further, the question of x² + ny² leads all the way to quadratic reciprocity and class field theory.

- And there's Lagrange's four-square theorem: every positive integer is a sum of four squares.  
  还有拉格朗日四平方和定理：每个正整数都是四个平方数之和。
- Now applications. First, Pythagorean triples: a prime can be the hypotenuse of a right triangle with integer sides if and only if it's one mod four.

- For example, thirteen equals three squared plus two squared, which gives the triangle five, twelve, thirteen.

  最令人惊讶的是圆周率。

- Remainders of primes mod four turn out to be connected to pi.

## 10　模拟面试 · Mock Interview

### 中文版：讲解
- 下面进入模拟面试。追问一般分四层：陈述、证明、条件、延伸。屏幕上是英文问答，我用中文讲每个问题在考什么，以及怎样回答更自然。
- 第一问通常是开放式的。先用一句话讲清定理，再用一句话说你为什么喜欢它，比如“陈述简单，证明却连接了数论、几何和组合”。不要一上来就背证明。
- 第二问考数学上的准确性：要说出“奇素数”“当且仅当”“模四余一”。最好主动补充二和合数的情形，表明你清楚定理的边界。
- 第三问一句话就够：平方数模四只能余零或一。答完立刻指出“难的是反方向”，把话题引向你准备好的证明。
- 第四问要在一分钟内讲清结构：集合 S、两个对合、奇偶性。用上 involution、fixed point、parity 这几个词，并主动说出那个唯一的不动点。
- 第五问最能拉开差距：素数用在哪里？要答出两处：边界情形，以及不动点的唯一性。再举二十一的反例：两个不动点，奇偶论证失效，而二十一确实不是平方和。
- 第六问考你是不是真的理解：怎么会想到这个映射？回答风车图：同一个轮廓恰好有两种切法。这一问答好了，体现的是理解，而不是背诵。
- 第七问问能不能构造。分两层回答：证明本身给出一条路径算法；更快的方法是先求负一模 p 的平方根，再做辗转相除。能举出十三的例子会很加分。
- 第八问问唯一性：回答“在顺序和正负号意义下唯一”，理由是高斯整数的唯一分解。
- 第九问问推广。列出三个方向：合数、其他二次型、四平方和。最后坦诚地说类域论还没学过，但很好奇。诚实比硬撑更好。
- 第十问问应用。勾股数和圆周率的莱布尼茨级数是两个好例子，后者尤其让人印象深刻。
- 第十一问是个人思考：如果只能带走一个想法？说出“用奇偶性证明存在”，再联系握手引理和斯潘纳引理，展示知识之间的联系。
- 最后可能来一道即兴小题：2027 是不是平方和？它是模四余三的素数，所以不是；顺便提一句，2026 等于四十五的平方加一的平方。冷静、快速、准确，就是最好的收尾。
- 整体策略只有一条：每个回答先给结论，再给理由，最后留一个可以继续追问的钩子，让面试官顺着你准备好的方向问下去。

### English version: role-play（英文原文 + 逐句中文）

*Narrator*: Now let's put it all together in a mock interview. The questions come in four layers: statement, proof, conditions, and beyond. Listen to how each answer gives a conclusion first, then a reason, and then leaves a hook for the next question.

> 现在把这些整合成一场模拟面试。问题分四层：陈述、证明、条件、延伸。注意每个回答都是先给结论，再给理由，然后为下一个问题留一个钩子。

**Q (考官)**: So, what's your favorite theorem?

> 那么，你最喜欢的定理是什么？

**A (考生)**: I'd say Fermat's two-square theorem. It says that an odd prime is a sum of two squares exactly when it's one mod four. What I love is the contrast: the statement is something you could explain to a middle-school student, but the proofs touch number theory, geometry, and even combinatorics.

> 我会说是费马平方和定理。它说一个奇素数是两个平方数之和，当且仅当它模四余一。我喜欢的是其中的反差：陈述连初中生都能听懂，证明却涉及数论、几何，甚至组合数学。

**Q (考官)**: Can you state it precisely? What happens with two, or with composite numbers?

> 你能准确陈述吗？二或者合数的情况呢？

**A (考生)**: Sure. For an odd prime p, p = a² + b² for some integers a and b if and only if p is congruent to one mod four. Two is also fine, since it's one plus one. For composite numbers, the rule is: every prime factor that's three mod four has to appear to an even power. So forty-five works, but twenty-one doesn't.

> 当然。对奇素数 p，存在整数 a、b 使 p = a² + b²，当且仅当 p ≡ 1 (mod 4)。二也可以，因为它等于一加一。对合数，规则是：每个模四余三的素因子都必须出现偶数次。所以 45 可以，21 不行。

**Q (考官)**: Why is 'one mod four' necessary?

> 为什么“模四余一”是必要的？

**A (考生)**: Because squares are only zero or one mod four. So a sum of two squares is zero, one, or two mod four, never three. That direction takes one line. The interesting part is the converse.

> 因为平方数模四只能是零或一。所以两平方和模四是零、一或二，绝不会是三。这个方向一行就够了。有趣的是逆命题。

**Q (考官)**: Okay. Can you sketch a proof of the converse, in about a minute?

> 好。你能在一分钟左右概述一下逆命题的证明吗？

**A (考生)**: I'll use Zagier's proof. Consider the set S of positive integer triples with x² + 4yz = p. It's finite. On S there's an obvious involution: swap y and z. Its fixed points give p = x² + (2y)². Zagier found a second involution with exactly one fixed point, namely (1, 1, (p − 1)/4). Since an involution pairs up elements, the size of S has the same parity as its number of fixed points. So S has odd size, and the swap must have a fixed point too.

> 我用扎吉尔的证明。考虑满足 x² + 4yz = p 的正整数三元组构成的集合 S，它是有限的。S 上有一个显然的对合：交换 y 和 z。它的不动点给出 p = x² + (2y)²。扎吉尔找到了第二个对合，它恰好有一个不动点，即 (1, 1, (p − 1)/4)。因为对合把元素两两配对，S 的大小与不动点个数奇偶相同。所以 S 的大小是奇数，交换也必须有不动点。

**Q (考官)**: Where exactly did you use that p is prime?

> 你到底在哪里用到了 p 是素数？

**A (考生)**: In two places. First, to make Zagier's map well defined: in the boundary cases, p would be a perfect square or a multiple of four. Second, to show the fixed point is unique: a fixed point means p = x(x + 4z), and primality forces x to be one. For example, with twenty-one there are two fixed points, coming from 21 = 1 × 21 = 3 × 7. So the parity argument breaks down, and indeed, twenty-one is not a sum of two squares.

> 在两个地方。第一，保证扎吉尔映射有定义：在边界情形，p 会是完全平方数或四的倍数。第二，证明不动点唯一：不动点意味着 p = x(x + 4z)，素性迫使 x = 1。例如对 21，有两个不动点，分别来自 21 = 1 × 21 和 21 = 3 × 7。所以奇偶论证失效，而 21 确实不是两个平方数之和。

**Q (考官)**: That map looks like a trick. How would anyone come up with it?

> 那个映射看起来像个技巧。怎么会有人想到它？

**A (考生)**: That was my first reaction too. The answer is a picture. x² + 4yz is the area of a windmill: a square with four rectangular blades. The outline of a windmill can be cut into a square and four blades in exactly two ways, and Zagier's map just switches between them. Once you see the picture, the formula feels inevitable.

> 我第一次看到时也是这个反应。答案是一幅图。x² + 4yz 是一架风车的面积：一个正方形加四片长方形风叶。风车的轮廓恰好有两种方式切成一个正方形和四片风叶，扎吉尔的映射就是在两者之间切换。一旦看到这幅图，公式就显得水到渠成。

**Q (考官)**: Your proof shows existence. How would you actually find a and b for a large prime?

> 你的证明只说明了存在。对一个大素数，你实际上怎么求出 a 和 b？

**A (考生)**: There are two answers. The proof itself gives an algorithm: alternate the two involutions, starting from the cross-shaped windmill, and you'll walk to a solution. But that can take a long time. A much faster method is to find a square root of minus one mod p, and run the Euclidean algorithm on p and that number. The first remainder below the square root of p gives a, and then b follows. For thirteen: five squared is minus one mod thirteen, the first small remainder is three, and thirteen is nine plus four.

> 有两个答案。证明本身给出一个算法：从十字形风车出发交替使用两个对合，就会走到一个解。但这可能很慢。快得多的方法是先求出 −1 模 p 的平方根，再对 p 和这个数做辗转相除。第一个小于 √p 的余数给出 a，b 随之得出。以 13 为例：5² ≡ −1 (mod 13)，第一个小余数是 3，而 13 = 9 + 4。

**Q (考官)**: Is the representation unique?

> 这种表示是唯一的吗？

**A (考生)**: Yes, up to order and signs. The cleanest reason is that the Gaussian integers have unique factorization. Writing a² + b² = (a + bi)(a − bi), a prime that's one mod four splits into exactly two conjugate Gaussian primes, so there's essentially only one way.

> 是的，在不计顺序和正负号的意义下唯一。最干净的理由是高斯整数有唯一分解。把 a² + b² 写成 (a + bi)(a − bi)，一个模四余一的素数恰好分裂成两个共轭的高斯素数，所以本质上只有一种写法。

**Q (考官)**: How would you generalize the theorem?

> 你会怎样推广这个定理？

**A (考生)**: There are several directions. One is composite numbers, using the identity for products of sums of two squares, which is really multiplication of complex numbers. Another is other forms, like x² + 2y², which works for primes that are one or three mod eight. And Lagrange proved that every positive integer is a sum of four squares. The deepest direction is x² + ny² in general, which leads to class field theory. I haven't studied that yet, but it's something I'm really curious about.

> 有几个方向。一个是合数，用“平方和之积”的恒等式，它本质上就是复数乘法。另一个是其他二次型，比如 x² + 2y²，适用于模八余一或余三的素数。拉格朗日还证明了每个正整数都是四个平方数之和。最深的方向是一般的 x² + ny²，它通向类域论。我还没学过，但非常好奇。

**Q (考官)**: Is the theorem useful for anything beyond itself?

> 这个定理在它自身之外还有什么用吗？

**A (考生)**: One example I like is Pythagorean triples: a prime is the hypotenuse of an integer right triangle exactly when it's one mod four. A more surprising one is pi. Counting lattice points in a circle with the two-square formula gives the Leibniz series: π/4 = 1 − 1/3 + 1/5 − ….

> 我喜欢的一个例子是勾股数：一个素数是整数直角三角形的斜边，当且仅当它模四余一。更令人惊讶的是圆周率：用两平方和公式数圆内格点，可以得到莱布尼茨级数 π/4 = 1 − 1/3 + 1/5 − …

**Q (考官)**: If you had to keep only one idea from this proof, what would it be?

> 如果只能从这个证明里带走一个想法，你会选什么？

**A (考生)**: Probably the parity trick: to prove that something exists, show that the number of such things is odd. It's surprisingly general. The same idea appears in the handshake lemma and in Sperner's lemma. And what I find beautiful is that a counting argument, which seems totally non-constructive, can be turned into an algorithm by following the pairs.

> 大概是奇偶技巧：要证明某个东西存在，就证明这类东西的个数是奇数。它出奇地通用：同样的想法出现在握手引理和斯潘纳引理里。我觉得美的是：一个看似完全非构造的计数论证，可以通过沿着配对走下去而变成算法。

**Q (考官)**: Quick one: is 2027 a sum of two squares?

> 快问一个：2027 是两个平方数之和吗？

**A (考生)**: No. 2027 is a prime, and it's three mod four, so it's impossible. But 2026 is: it's forty-five squared plus one squared.

> 不是。2027 是素数，而且模四余三，所以不可能。但 2026 是：它等于 45² + 1²。

*Narrator*: Notice the pattern: conclusion first, then the reason, then a hook. That's how you lead the conversation toward what you know best.

> 注意这个模式：先结论，再理由，然后留钩子。这样你就能把对话引向你最擅长的方向。
